Research

Post-graduation uncertainty among university students

Research assistant, Bilkent University Department of Economics · Supervisor: Dr. Mustafa Eray Yücel · 2026–present

I conducted exploratory interviews and synthesized recurring themes into 32 statements for a planned co-authored paper. The next stage uses Q methodology to study how participants rank those statements and identify shared viewpoints.

Projects

AI exposure and inequality of opportunity in Türkiye

GE 400 graduation project · Proposal in development · 2026

This proposed study examines whether access to higher-paying, AI-complementary occupations varies with regional AI exposure and circumstances such as region and family background. The planned approach compares regions using opportunity-inequality measures.

Econometrics: model specification and diagnostics

ECON 301 · Stata · Spring 2026

Using a 250-observation dataset, I compared OLS specifications, assessed variable selection and multicollinearity with joint tests and VIF, tested for heteroskedasticity, and estimated a weighted least squares model.
